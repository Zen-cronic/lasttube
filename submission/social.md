## LinkedIn

What do you do when the exact lipstick shade you know works is discontinued?

I built **LastTube** for the DevNetwork [API + Cloud + AI] Hackathon 2026. SerpApi turns a
timestamped Google Shopping response into exact listing text, merchant, price, observed offer, and
evidence. Images below a disclosed 10% heuristic coverage floor are rejected. Perfect Corp Makeup
VTO must successfully render the remembered shade baseline and passing candidates on the same
sample face. Every shortlist row becomes system-excluded or human-decided; preference is
accepted-only and CIE76 does not decide.

In the recorded path, Ngozi is system-excluded at 2.5% coverage; the human rejects lower-ΔE ABH,
accepts and prefers NYX, and visibly overrides the metric. The three-candidate ledger is complete,
but variant and shade are absent while source-image and candidate-lifecycle evidence are missing.
The final card says `No actionable lead yet` and never presents a Buy action or formulation claim.

The build is deliberately honest about its boundaries: listings are observed evidence rather than
stock guarantees; merchant-image color and ΔE are explanation aids; fixture playback stays visibly
labeled; and the proof inventory distinguishes the receipted search and lost-shade baseline from
metadata-only candidate outputs.

## Follow-up proof post

The part of LastTube I care about most is its truth boundary. The stable demo is labeled FIXTURE:
40 normalized SerpApi candidates and the lost-shade Perfect Corp lifecycle have receipts; candidate
outputs retain hashes, bytes, task ids, and poll counts while declaring missing request/lifecycle
fields. Inspect the inventory:
https://github.com/Zen-cronic/lasttube/tree/main/proofs

New live candidates use a versioned per-run bundle that re-hashes retained sanitized search and
Perfect response artifacts, source/output bytes, and URL lineage before review. Offline tamper tests
prove the gate fails closed. It is not durable storage on the current ephemeral host, does not
configure a live SKU enrichment source, and does not retroactively upgrade the fixtures.
